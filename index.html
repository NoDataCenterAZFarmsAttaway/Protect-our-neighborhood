A Message to Our Neighbors

Our community deserves to remain a residential haven. The proposed industrial expansion threatens the peace, safety, and character of our homes.

The Real Impact: What They Aren't Telling You

  • ✕ 24/7 Acoustic Pollution: Industrial cooling fans create a constant "low-frequency hum" that travels through walls and causes chronic stress.
  • ✕ Water Depletion: In our desert climate, these facilities consume millions of gallons of water daily, straining our local water table.
  • ✕ Utility Rate Hikes: Residents often bear the cost of the massive electrical infrastructure upgrades needed for industrial loads.
  • ✕ Safety Hazards: Battery storage (BESS) introduces risks of chemical fires and toxic off-gassing near residential zones.
  • ✕ Property Devaluation: Industrial blighting and transmission lines can decrease home values by 10-15%.
