The Real Impact: What They Aren't Telling You

  • ✕
    24/7 Noise Pollution: Constant low-frequency hum from industrial cooling fans linked to sleep deprivation and chronic stress.
  • ✕
    Water Depletion: These facilities can consume millions of gallons of water daily in our vulnerable desert environment.
  • ✕
    Utility Rate Hikes: Residents often bear the cost of infrastructure upgrades needed for massive industrial power loads.
  • ✕
    Safety Hazards: Battery storage (BESS) introduces risks of chemical fires and toxic off-gassing near residential zones.
  • ✕
    Property Devaluation: Industrial blighting and transmission lines can decrease nearby home values by 10-15%.
